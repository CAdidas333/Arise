Where we are

V18 is complete and the 3D view has caught up with the 2D one; everything below is live at cadidas333.github.io/Arise and /3d/ (stamp 20261020150450). All of it was built by free workers and checked by test scripts, a balance bot and captured frames at desktop and phone sizes. Nobody has played it by hand, tried it on a real phone, or heard the music, and by the bot it is still too easy. Difficulty waits on Chris's play-through.

What's built (15 to 20 Oct)

  • ✅ Three-phase boss fights: a scripted move at half and a last stand at a quarter, for all six bosses
  • ✅ Floor hazards that stay: webs and rubble slow, fire and blood burn
  • ✅ 3D view at parity: health bars, ARISE prompt, boss arrow, low-health edge, glowing shots
  • ✅ 3D look: minimap, thick walls with a cutaway, torches, rubble, shadows, motes, warning rims
  • ✅ Kevin's backpack: 24 slots, sell weaker gear, UPGRADE tags, a full-bag check
  • ✅ The ARISE rise: a raised shadow climbs out of a dark pool
  • ✅ The System's level-up window (violet for a rank-up)
  • ✅ The stamp script refuses a broken build

Known issues / gaps

  • ⚠️ Never on a real phone: frame rate, the two-finger 3D camera, the share sheet and music memory are unmeasured.

Key technical notes

One global scope. 16 classic scripts share it; a name declared twice stops a whole script. tools/stamp.sh now checks names, parses every script and loads them in Node before it stamps.
Two views, one game. 3d/index.html is generated from index.html; never edit it. The 3D page swaps the 2D drawing scripts for 3d/render3d.js; js/12b-overlay.js is shared.
Testing in the hidden browser pane. Drive the game with step(1/60) and draw(); wait out the 3.2-second boss intro; never let a test save on the public site.
Rules from Chris. Free rein (decide and ship). His: money, and the art rule: no star symbols, runes, occult or religious imagery, in pictures or words.
